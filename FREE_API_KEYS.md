## 💡 TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS

1. **Be Specific**: Include exact historical periods, locations, and details
2. **Scene Length**: Keep individual scenes 5-10 seconds for Google Flow
3. **Consistency**: Use same character descriptions across all scenes
4. **Camera Work**: Specify camera movements (pan, zoom, tracking shot)
5. **Lighting**: Mention time of day, weather, indoor/outdoor
6. **Sound**: Request specific sound effects and ambient noise
7. **Batch Generate**: Create all scene descriptions first, then generate videos
8. **Daily Limits**: Spread video generation across days to maximize free credits
