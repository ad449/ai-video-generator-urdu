📋 PROJECT SUMMARY:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A complete, production-ready web application that automatically 
generates professional Urdu historical story videos using free AI APIs.

Type a story idea → Get complete Urdu story → Generate videos → Download!


🎯 WHAT IT DOES:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

✅ Generates complete Urdu stories from simple prompts
✅ Converts stories to video prompts automatically
✅ Creates professional videos (768x512, 5sec, MP4)
✅ Supports multiple story types (Historical, Cultural, Fantasy)
✅ Downloads all videos with one click
✅ Retry failed videos automatically
✅ Manual mode for zero-cost generation


💰 COST & PERFORMANCE:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Story Generation:     FREE (Groq API - 14,400 req/day)
Video Generation:     $5 FREE credits (FAL.ai) = 10-20 videos
After Free Credits:   ~$0.10-0.30 per video
Manual Mode:          FREE (Google Flow - 50 videos/day)

3 Scenes:  ~2 minutes | $0.30-0.90
5 Scenes:  ~3 minutes | $0.50-1.50
8 Scenes:  ~5 minutes | $0.80-2.40

🚀 QUICK START:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Open start.html in browser
2. Click "Launch App"
3. Get free API keys:
   - Groq: https://console.groq.com
   - FAL.ai: https://fal.ai/dashboard
4. Paste keys in app
5. Enter story idea: "میر تقی میر کی زندگی"
6. Select: 3 scenes, Historical Story
7. Click: Generate Story & Video
8. Wait: ~2 minutes
9. Download: Your videos!

🐛 TROUBLESHOOTING:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Problem: Video generation failed
Solution: Click "Retry Generation" or check API key

Problem: Out of credits
Solution: Switch to "Manual" mode, use Google Flow (50 free/day)

Problem: Story quality low
Solution: Be more specific in input, add details

Problem: API key not working
Solution: Verify key is correct, check API limits
